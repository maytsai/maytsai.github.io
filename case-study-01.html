Case Study 01 · Infillion Catalina

Turning Customer Behavior Into a Growth Opportunity

From transaction data to a targeted strategy that generated $50K in incremental revenue at 2.5:1 ROAS.

§ 1The Challenge

The business wanted to identify opportunities to drive incremental customer value through more targeted offers and programs.

Rather than starting with a predefined campaign, I approached the problem from the customer side:

The questionWhat can transaction behavior tell us about where the biggest opportunity exists?

The goal was to identify meaningful behavioral patterns, determine which customers represented the strongest opportunity, and translate the findings into an actionable strategy.

§ 2My Approach
01

Discover the Opportunity

I started with customer transaction data to understand how customers were behaving across key dimensions such as purchase behavior, engagement, and customer value.

Rather than looking only at overall averages, I segmented customers to identify meaningful differences in behavior and uncover where an intervention could potentially drive incremental value.

Key questionWhere are we seeing customer behaviors that suggest an opportunity for targeted intervention?
02

Define the Target

Once the opportunity was identified, I translated the behavioral insight into a practical targeting strategy.

I evaluated the characteristics of customers who represented the strongest opportunity and worked through how the audience could be defined for activation.

This required balancing analytical precision with business constraints — the goal was not simply to identify the “best” customers, but to define an audience that was both meaningful and actionable.

03

Size the Opportunity

Before activation, I helped quantify the potential opportunity by estimating the size of the target audience and aligning the opportunity with expected business investment.

This connected the analytical recommendation to the questions the business actually needed to answer:

  • How many households could we reach?
  • What level of investment would be required?
  • What return would make the strategy worthwhile?
  • How should the opportunity be prioritized?
04

Activate & Measure

I partnered with cross-functional teams to translate the analytical strategy into an actionable customer program.

Once activated, I analyzed performance against the appropriate business metrics to determine whether the strategy generated incremental value.

The analysis was not treated as a one-time recommendation — measurement was used to evaluate the outcome and inform what should happen next.

§ 3The Impact
$50K
Incremental revenue
2.5:1
ROAS

The analysis turned a behavioral pattern in transaction data into a measurable customer growth opportunity and provided a data-driven basis for targeting and investment decisions.

§ 4What I Took Away

The most valuable part of the analysis was not simply identifying a customer segment. It was connecting the entire decision process:

Behavior→Opportunity→Target→Investment→Activation→Measurement

For me, customer analytics is most valuable when it moves beyond describing what customers did and helps answer:

The real questionWhat should we do differently because of what the data tells us?
